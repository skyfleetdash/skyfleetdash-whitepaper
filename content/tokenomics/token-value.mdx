# Token Value Management

**Total Supply of SkyFleet Dash Token (SFDT)**
1 billion tokens

**Initial Circulating Supply**
10% of total supply (100 million SFDT tokens)

---

| **Aspect** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| **Initial Token Price** | $0.10 per SFDT |
| **Presale Price** | $0.08 per SFDT (3 rounds, 100M tokens, $8M hard cap) |
| **Initial Circulating Supply at TGE** | ~135 million SFDT (presale TGE unlock + public launch + community initial release). Locked liquidity tokens (100M) are excluded per industry standards. |
| **Initial Market Cap** | at $0.10, ~$13.5 million (circulating supply x token price) |
| **Fully Diluted Market Cap** | $100 million (1 billion tokens x $0.10) |

**Deflationary Note** — Due to the 2% burn tax on every transfer, total supply decreases continuously. The fully diluted valuation will increase over time as burned tokens are permanently removed from supply.

> This tokenomics model is designed to support SkyFleet Dash's ecosystem, balancing user rewards with sustainable revenue streams and supply management, thereby enhancing the long-term value of SFDT.
