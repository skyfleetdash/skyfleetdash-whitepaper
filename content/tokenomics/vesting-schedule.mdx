# Token Release and Vesting Schedule

To ensure stability and sustainable growth, SFDT follows a structured release schedule.

---

| **Allocation** | **Percentage** | **Token Amount** | **Vesting Details** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Presale** | 10% | 100 million | $0.05/token. 20% at TGE, 1-month cliff, remaining 80% vests monthly over 4 months. Fully unlocked in 5 months. |
| **Public Sale** | 10% | 100 million | $0.10/token. Released at launch via exchange listings. |
| **Team Allocation** | 10% | 100 million | 1-year cliff, vesting over 2 years |
| **Advisors** | 5% | 50 million | 6-month cliff, vesting over 1 year |
| **Staking and Rewards** | 15% | 150 million | Dynamic APY: 25% (< 10M staked) to 5% (> 200M staked). Lock bonuses: +2% (3mo), +5% (6mo), +8% (12mo). Pool replenished by 50% of transaction fees. |
| **Game Rewards & Incentives** | 15% | 150 million | Annual emission: 5% of pool (Yr 1), scaling to 10% (Yr 3), tapering to 5% (Yr 6+). Daily cap enforced. Pool sustains 20+ years. |
| **Exchange Liquidity** | 10% | 100 million | Deposited into PancakeSwap LP at launch. LP tokens locked via Team Finance for 12+ months. Not included in circulating supply. |
| **Development Fund** | 10% | 100 million | Gradual release over time based on development milestones |
| **Partnerships & Collaborations** | 10% | 100 million | Released based on partnership agreements and promotional activities |
| **Community Reserve** | 5% | 50 million | Used for community building, airdrops, and promotional campaigns |

---

**Initial Circulating Supply at TGE:**

> - Presale unlock: 20M SFDT (20% of 100M)
> - Public launch: 100M SFDT
> - Community unlock: 15M SFDT (5% of 300M)
> - Total circulating at TGE: ~135M SFDT
> - Liquidity pool tokens (100M) are locked and excluded from circulating supply per CoinGecko/CoinMarketCap standards
>
> **Initial Market Cap at $0.10:** ~$13.5M (circulating) | $100M (fully diluted)
