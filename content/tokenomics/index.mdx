## What is SkyFleet Dash Token (SFDT)?

SkyFleet Dash Token (SFDT) is the Foundation of the SkyFleet Dash ecosystem, serving as both a utility and governance token that empowers players, investors, and the community. As the core currency within the platform, SFDT drives the in-game economy, enabling players to access exclusive features, participate in competitive gameplay, and shape the future of the platform through governance.

Within the SkyFleet Dash platform, SFDT is primarily used to access various gameplay features. Players use SFDT to enter competitive races, purchase in-game items such as spaceships, weapons, and equipment, and enhance their avatars with powerful upgrades. This play-to-earn model allows players to earn more SFDT through performance in matches and tournaments, creating a dynamic and rewarding gaming experience. Beyond gameplay, SFDT serves as the foundation of the platform's governance system. As a governance token, SFDT enables holders to participate in decision-making through a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO). Token holders can vote on critical platform decisions, including game feature implementations, system upgrades, and major development plans. For those who prefer not to participate directly, the platform also allows for delegated voting, ensuring that player voices are always represented.

SkyFleet Dash has two fee mechanisms:
- An on-chain 2% burn tax on every SFDT transfer, permanently removing tokens from supply.
- A platform-level 5% marketplace fee on asset trades, split equally — 2.5% to the staking reward pool and 2.5% to platform operations. This dual mechanism creates both deflationary pressure and sustainable revenue.

The SkyFleet Dash Foundation plays a crucial role in supporting the ecosystem's growth. The foundation provides grants and funding to game creators and community-driven projects, fostering innovation and expanding the platform's value. This commitment to continuous development ensures that SkyFleet Dash remains a dynamic and player-centric environment, driven by its community.
