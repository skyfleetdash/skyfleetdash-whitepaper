# Player Market

In the spacecraft racing game genre, many players invest real-world money into their vehicles, upgrades, and in-game items to enhance their racing experience. Traditional games do not offer any means of recovering this investment if players stop playing or if the game shuts down. This is a common issue with free-to-play (F2P) models, where purchases are non-transferable and become worthless once a player exits the game.

In SkyFleetDash, players benefit from a blockchain-powered ownership model, ensuring that their investments in in-game assets are secure and transferable. Unlike traditional free-to-play (F2P) games where assets are permanently lost, SkyFleetDash grants players full ownership of their spacecraft, parts, and upgrades through SkyFleetDash Tokens (SFDT). These assets can be freely traded or sold to other players, even if a player decides to leave the game, allowing them to recover their investment. The platform also features a robust marketplace where players can buy, sell, or rent spacecraft and custom parts, leveraging a peer-to-peer system that extends asset value beyond the game. Additionally, players can compete in high-stakes racing tournaments, earning SFDT rewards and further enhancing the real-world value of their gaming experience.

> By addressing the limitations of conventional F2P models, SkyFleetDash empowers players to maintain and capitalize on the value of their purchases, creating a more sustainable and rewarding gaming ecosystem for spacecraft racing enthusiasts.
