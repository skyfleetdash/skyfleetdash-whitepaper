# A Competitive Gaming Ecosystem

SkyFleetDash is built around a dynamic competitive gaming ecosystem that allows players to engage in high-stakes multiplayer games while earning real-world rewards. Our platform is designed to cater to players who enjoy strategy, competition, and the thrill of earning tangible rewards through skill-based gameplay.

![Competitive Gaming](/img/4.png)

### Multiplayer Competitions

SkyFleetDash offers a wide range of multiplayer modes, where players can compete against others in real-time. Whether in solo matches or team-based tournaments, players can earn SkyFleetDash Tokens (SFDT) as rewards for winning. These tokens can be redeemed for in-game rewards and real-world value through SFDT.

### Tournament System

Tournaments in SkyFleetDash are always open, allowing players to compete in events using GameCoins (GC). Tournament buy-ins are tiered by player level: New players can wager up to 500 GC per race, while VIP players can wager up to 50,000 GC per race. Winners earn from the prize pool, with a percentage allocated to sustain the ecosystem. GC earned from tournament winnings (Earned GC) can be converted to SFDT at the base rate of 1,000 GC = 1 SFDT.

### In-Game Economy

The economy of SkyFleetDash revolves around a dual-token system. GameCoins (GC) are the in-game currency earned through gameplay, while SFDT is the blockchain utility token. Players maintain two GC wallets: Bonus GC (from signups, referrals, promotions — usable in-game only) and Earned GC (from multiplayer race winnings — convertible to SFDT). The base conversion rate is 1,000 GC = 1 SFDT, with dynamic adjustment up to 2,500:1 during high-demand periods. Per-user daily conversion caps are enforced by tier: New players (5 SFDT/day), Active (20 SFDT/day), Veteran (50 SFDT/day), VIP (100 SFDT/day). A global daily conversion pool cap of 0.1% of circulating SFDT prevents mass conversion events. Every SFDT transfer is subject to a 2% automatic burn tax hardcoded in the smart contract, creating continuous deflationary pressure.

### Betting and Wagering

SkyFleetDash allows players to wager their Earned GC in multiplayer modes, raising the stakes and adding excitement to each match. Wager caps are enforced by player tier to ensure responsible gameplay: New (500 GC), Active (5,000 GC), Veteran (25,000 GC), VIP (50,000 GC) per race. By wagering on themselves, players can multiply their rewards based on their performance, creating a competitive atmosphere where high skill is incentivized. SkyFleetDash operates as a skill-based gaming platform under Seychelles jurisdiction.
