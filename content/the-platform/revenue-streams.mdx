# Revenue Streams

SkyFleetDash has five primary revenue streams:

**Tournament Entry Fees** — A percentage of all GC wagered in tournaments is retained as platform revenue

**Marketplace Commissions** — 5% fee on all marketplace transactions (2.5% to staking pool, 2.5% to platform operations)

**GC-to-SFDT Conversion Fees** — 2% burn tax on every SFDT transfer is hardcoded in the smart contract

**Premium Content & Subscriptions** — VIP access, exclusive items, and premium game modes

**Partnerships & Sponsorships** — Brand collaborations, sponsored tournaments, and advertising placements

Revenue allocation across the four stakeholder pools (Foundation, Staking, Treasury, Reserve) is governed by smart contracts and will transition to DAO governance over time.
