# Development and Evolution

The idea for SkyFleet Dash took shape in *2023* when the development team, consisting of blockchain enthusiasts and experienced game designers, identified a gap in the competitive gaming market. They recognized the growing popularity of decentralized platforms and the potential to integrate these systems into a gamified environment.

SkyFleet Dash's core concept is built around a dual-token economy: GameCoins (GC) serve as the in-game currency earned through skilled gameplay, while SkyFleet Dash Tokens (SFDT) are the blockchain utility token on BNB Smart Chain. Earned GC converts to SFDT at a base rate of 1,000:1, with a 2% burn tax on every SFDT transfer creating deflationary tokenomics. The game also introduced a decentralized governance model, enabling players to have a say in future developments through a DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization).
