# Roadmap

- Platform concept and game design finalized
- Core team assembled — blockchain developers, game designers, and strategists
- SkyFleetDash Ltd incorporated in Seychelles (License No: 242752)

- SFDT smart contract developed and deployed on BNB Smart Chain

- Beta testing with early community

- Platform bug fixes and UX improvements

- Conversion model, emission schedule, staking APY, and governance framework finalized

- Website and whitepaper comprehensive updates
